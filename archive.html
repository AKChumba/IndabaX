IndabaX Namibia 2025

Theme: "AI for Sustainable Development"

University of Namibia, Windhoek • March 15-16, 2025

Our inaugural IndabaX Namibia brought together over 200 participants from across the country and region. The event focused on how AI can contribute to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals in the African context.
