About IndabaX Namibia

Empowering Namibia's AI future through education, community, and innovation

What is IndabaX?

The Deep Learning Indaba is a movement to strengthen African Machine Learning by bringing together African researchers, practitioners, and students to learn, share, and grow together.

IndabaX Namibia serves as the premier AI conference in our nation, fostering collaboration between academia, industry, and government to accelerate AI adoption and development.

Global Movement

Connecting Namibian AI talent to the broader African and global AI community

Our Mission

Building Namibia's AI ecosystem through three core pillars

Education

Providing accessible, world-class AI education to students, researchers, and professionals across Namibia. We believe in democratizing knowledge and making AI education available to all.

Community

Building a strong, inclusive community of AI enthusiasts, practitioners, and researchers. We foster collaboration, mentorship, and knowledge sharing across all levels of expertise.

Innovation

Encouraging innovation and entrepreneurship in AI. We support local startups, research projects, and initiatives that leverage AI to solve uniquely Namibian and African challenges.
